# Supported Platform Fields

## Platforms

| Platform | Runtime Key | Core Fields |
|---|---|---|
| WeChat official account | `wechat` | title, article excerpt, link, published time, reads, likes, comments, shares |
| Xiaohongshu | `xhs` | title, body excerpt, link, published time, likes, comments, saves, shares |

## Required Provenance

- source URL or authorized export path
- collection time
- platform key
- title and available content excerpt
- evidence status and human review status

Do not infer unavailable metrics or content details from a title, filename or platform name.
